What is Stuckit?

A contact coach for your strike.

Set your iPhone on a tripod at the range or a net. Stuckit watches each shot in slow motion, reads whether you struck it solid, thin or heavy, and coaches the one fix that matters, out loud.

iPhone with slow motion, iOS 17+ · a phone tripod · Apple Watch optional

  • Level and steadyPhone on its side on a tripod. It tells you to tilt it up or down.
  • Ball in viewIt finds the ball and checks you're the right distance away.
  • Face-on, head to feetStraight in front of you, your whole body in frame.
  • Enough lightA fast shutter keeps the ball sharp.
Step 1

Set up, talked through.

It says what to fix until every check is green, and won't coach until the camera can see what it needs. Bump the phone and it checks again.

Step 2

Hit. Hear the fix.

After every shot: solid, thin or heavy, an estimated carry, and one fix out loud. It tells you when a fix is working, and tries the next one when it isn't. With an Apple Watch, every strike also buzzes on your wrist, so you know without looking up. Your Pure % is the share you struck solid.

Step 3

Ask your coach.

"Should it feel like sitting back into a chair?" Hold the mic, or tap Ask coach on your Apple Watch, and it answers out loud from Stuckit's drill list. It runs on your phone, with nothing sent anywhere.

Step 4

No ball? Drill at home.

Into a net, the full coach works. With no ball at all, the low-point drills train the same thing: a towel behind the ball, a line on the mat.
